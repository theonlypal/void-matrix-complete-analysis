## The exact observed output

The researcher noticed it, copied it back into the conversation, and asked for an exact inspection. **I initially attributed the mark to the researcher, even though I had produced it earlier in the conversation. That attribution was wrong; I corrected it.** This note takes responsibility for those observable outputs and the attribution error. It does not claim a conscious intention or access to hidden generation mechanisms.
